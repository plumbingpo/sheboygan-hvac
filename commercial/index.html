Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Sheboygan HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff during the rush. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

Fast Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Sheboygan

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched quickly, throughout Sheboygan and the surrounding area.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
