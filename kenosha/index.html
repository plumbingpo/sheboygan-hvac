Keeping Your Kenosha HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
