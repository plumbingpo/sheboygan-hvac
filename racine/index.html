Keeping Your Racine HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement.
